George Boedecker is the co-founder of Crocs, and in this book, he tells the story of his life, his work, and his adventures both leading up to Crocs and since then. His motto seems to be to live life to the fullest, and it shows.

On the plus side, the book is an easy read and full of interesting anecdotes from Boedecker's life. He's definitely experienced *a lot!* On the other hand, it is incredibly poorly written and would have benefited immensely from some (more) professional editing. The sentences are mostly readable, but most of the stories lack a decent introduction or a wrap-up. It's a long list of "here's a thing that happened" mixed with half-hearted self-reflection.

Boedecker pretty much starts the book off saying you may not like him, and repeats that at least once a chapter, making it almost feel like a self-fulfilling prophecy. I gave it three stars because I liked reading about his experiences at Domino's (lots of it in SF) and the early days of Crocs, but your mileage may vary.
