I read the first six books in John Scalzi's *Old Man's War* series back in 2018 when a friend recommended them to me. I can't tell you much about what happened in them, but I recall reading them all one after the other and really liking them.

Published in 2025, a decade after *The End of All Things*, the sixth book in the series, *The Shattering Peace* is set 10 years after the events in book 6. It tells a standalone story centered around Gretchen Trujillo, who appeared in *The Last Colony* and *Zoe's Tale* and is now an adult. Gretchen gets pulled in to find a colony that has disappeared, which threatens the peace between civilizations. Together with her alien sidekick Ran, Gretchen discovers what happened, kicks alien ass, and... well, she doesn't chew bubblegum, but she *does* reconnect with her lover from the earlier books.

The book is well written, the plot is engaging and moves along rapidly, and the interactions with alien races are entertaining. Ran's interpretations and explanations provide comic relief throughout it all, and I chuckled regularly. I found the ending to the story a bit disappointing though. It's all a bit too convenient and lacks substance. A few extra pages on what actually happened would have gone a long way here. I'm starting to see a theme there. Scalzi has written another great page-turner, but here too... I doubt I'll remember much of it in a few years.
