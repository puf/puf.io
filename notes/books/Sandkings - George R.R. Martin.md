![Book cover of Sandkings by George R. R. Martin, featuring two large, insect-like creatures with colorful armored bodies and long, spindly legs crawling across an orange, rocky landscape.](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1564429105i/5393005.jpg)

A wealthy, arrogant man collects exotic pets and acquires a colony of *sandkings*, small, insect-like creatures that live in competing hives, build elaborate castles out of sand, and wage wars against each other. He initially enjoys watching the miniature civilizations evolve, but over time things spiral out of control as the creatures grow larger and smarter.

Sandkings packs a remarkably complete story into its 89 pages, and I enjoyed most of it. I'd give it 5 stars for its concept and the sci-fi/fantasy bits, but only 3 stars for the horror aspects of the story.
