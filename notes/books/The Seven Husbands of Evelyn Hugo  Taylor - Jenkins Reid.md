After not making it through the TV adaptation of [*Daisy Jones and the Six*][djs] a few years ago, I listened to the audiobook version of it earlier this year and *loved* it. *The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo* is my second foray into the writing of Taylor Jenkins Reid (TJR), and I wondered how it would compare.

Where Daisy Jones tells the story of a rock star in the 70s, Evelyn Hugo is a film star who rose to fame from the 50s to the 80s. Aside from her movies and looks, Evelyn is famous for the seven marriages she had over the years. In this book, she recruits a budding magazine writer to author her biography (to be released after her death), in which she tells all about those marriages, her rise to fame, and the forbidden love that shaped her life. Just like in *Daisy Jones*, there's a "big reveal" near the end of the book, and, once again, I found that much less shocking than I think was intended.

TJR writes compelling stories that explore interesting topics in settings that easily capture you (or me at least). While her books are slightly longer than average, they never feel that way. The story flows smoothly from phase to phase, and there are always plenty of things going on to keep you wondering. The audio narration is well done, though I did miss the full-cast narration of *Daisy Jones*. Let's call this a four-minus, rounded up. :-)
