I first encountered Florecita Panaderia, in its original location in a Bryant street alley. I'd been walking through the Inner Mission, wanted a pastry, but didn't feel like Tartine (whose large Manufactory is around the corner). Florecita's pan dulce was quite good, but somehow I only went there a few times.

![two pan dulce on a bag](https://i.postimg.cc/RV37pH1m/IMG-20261008-111155413-HDR-2.jpg)

Since September 2026 Florecita Panaderia has [relocated][articleonmove] to the [corner of 23rd street and Bartlett street][mapslink], in the location that used to host Shuggies (and Velvet Cantina before that). Florecita Bakery is attracting a lot of people to their new location; and for a good reason, as their made-from-scratch pan dulce are delicious!

The site contains two spaces, connected by a passage. You typically enter from the corner into the space where the concha's are sold and made.

![dough](https://i.postimg.cc/ZK98sdP5/IMG-20261008-114128904-HDR-2.jpg)
![rack](https://i.postimg.cc/m2cYpH3Z/IMG-20261008-114122127-HDR-2.jpg)

The dough is portioned on-site, and then baked into perfectly sized concha's right where you can see them. And smell them... oh my gosh, can you smell them!

![pantry](https://i.postimg.cc/MKc73Q0K/IMG-20261008-114109402-HDR-2.jpg)

After you get your concha(s), head to the second space to wait for your coffee and/or to relax and eat your pastry.

![mural](https://i.postimg.cc/rFd1ZrG8/IMG-20261008-113458079-HDR-2.jpg)

If the inside seating is all taken up (some folks were working on laptops on my last visit), there is also seating outside in the parklet.
