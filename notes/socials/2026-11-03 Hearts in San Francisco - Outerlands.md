When I happened to be at Union Square recently, I noticed that the [*Butterflight* heart][butterflight] on the southwest corner is gone and have been replaced by [*Outerlands* by artist Gina Teichert][outerlands].

![Heart-shaped statue with shops in the background](https://i.postimg.cc/FFjtksqq/image.png)

There isn't a lot of information about this one, but like all of them... it's pretty pretty. 😍
